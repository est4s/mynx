A real Debian on your phone: bash, apt and
thousands of programs. What you install stays.

Menu      type menu to come back to it.
Key bar   the buttons above the keyboard: Esc,
          Ctrl, Tab, arrows. Programs and games
          get their own bars. Swipe it for more.
Tabs      + opens one. Swipe across the text to
          change tabs; long-press one to rename.
Text size pinch the terminal.
Settings  menu → Settings: theme, font, key
          bars, the menu.
Files     menu → Files, or type files. Phone
          storage is /storage/emulated/0.
AI agents menu → AI agents installs Claude Code,
          Codex or Gemini CLI. Ask one to change
          your setup; it reads ~/AGENTS.md.
Again     menu → Getting started, or type
          pocket welcome.
